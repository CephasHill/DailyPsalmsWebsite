Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Overview

Daily Psalms is designed to help you read the Psalms and Proverbs without requiring an account. The current app is designed to work offline and does not include advertising.

Information stored on your device

The app stores information such as reading progress, streaks, selected reading path, translation preferences, and other app settings locally on your device. This information is used to provide the app's features and is not sent to us by the app.

Information we receive

We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to use Daily Psalms. If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and message you choose to send so we can respond.

Website information

This website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or analytics scripts. Like most websites, the hosting server may record basic technical request information as part of operating and securing the site.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe those changes. The date above indicates when this page was last revised.

Contact
